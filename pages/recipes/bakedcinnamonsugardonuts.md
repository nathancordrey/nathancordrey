# Baked Cinnamon Sugar Donuts

Soft, cake-style donuts baked in a donut pan, then dipped in butter and cinnamon sugar. No frying or stand mixer needed.

**Yield:** 8 donuts  
**Prep time:** 20 minutes  
**Bake time:** 9–10 minutes per batch  
**Total time:** About 45 minutes

Adapted from [Sally's Baking — Baked Cinnamon Sugar Donuts](https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/baked-cinnamon-sugar-donuts/) by Sally McKenney.

## Ingredients

### Donuts

- 1 cup (125 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1 large egg, at room temperature
- 1/3 cup (65 g) packed light or dark brown sugar
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) milk, at room temperature
- 1/4 cup (60 g) sour cream or plain yogurt, at room temperature
- 2 tbsp (28 g) unsalted butter, melted
- 1 1/2 tsp pure vanilla extract

### Cinnamon Sugar Topping

- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/2 cup (113 g) unsalted butter, melted

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to **350°F**. Grease a donut pan well. The batter makes eight donuts, so use two pans or bake in batches if you have a six-cavity pan.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, and nutmeg.
3. In another bowl, whisk the egg, brown sugar, milk, and sour cream (or yogurt) until smooth. Mix in the melted butter and vanilla.
4. Fold the wet mixture into the dry ingredients just until no dry flour remains. The finished batter should be thick; avoid overmixing.
5. Spoon the batter into a piping bag or zip-top bag with a corner snipped off. Pipe into the prepared donut cavities, filling them approximately **1/2 to 2/3 full**.
6. Bake for **9–10 minutes**, until the donuts gently spring back when touched. Let them rest in the pan for 2 minutes, then move them to a rack to cool slightly. If baking in batches, grease the pan again before using the remaining batter.
7. Stir together the granulated sugar and cinnamon in a bowl. Dip the slightly warm donuts in melted butter, then turn them in the cinnamon sugar until coated.
8. Serve soon after coating for the best texture.

## Notes

- **Equipment:** A donut pan works best. A zip-top bag makes the thick batter easy to pipe neatly into the cavities.
- **Muffin-pan option:** For about 8 muffins, fill greased standard muffin cups about 2/3 full. Bake at 350°F for approximately **18–20 minutes**, until a toothpick comes out clean. Dip the tops and sides in butter and cinnamon sugar.
- **Topping quantity:** The amounts above match Sally's original recipe and make a generous coating. You may have some left over.
- **Make ahead:** These donuts are best fresh. Keep leftovers covered at room temperature for 1–2 days, or in the refrigerator for up to 1 week.
- **Freezing:** Freeze uncoated baked donuts for up to 2–3 months. Thaw, warm briefly, and coat just before serving.
- **Doubling:** Double every ingredient for approximately 16 donuts.
