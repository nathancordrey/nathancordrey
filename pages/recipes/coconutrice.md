# Coconut Rice

Simple staple recipe for coconut rice.

## Ingredients
- 1.5 cups plain white rice (not jasmine)
- 1 can full fat coconut milk
- 1 cup water
- 1 heavy pinch of salt
- Cilantro (Optional)

## Instructions

1. Rinse the rice
2. Add all ingredients to a pot and bring to a boil
3. After boiling stir
4. Turn to simmer and wait 15 minutes.
5. Fluff with a fork and serve
6. (Optional) Add cilantro
