# Crockpot ranch chicken
A nice easy weeknight dinner.

## Ingredients

- 2 chicken breast
- 2 bell peppers yellow/red
- 1 yellow or sweet onion
- 1 ranch packet
- 1 splash water/chicken broth
  

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in a crockpot.
2. Cook on Low for 4 hours, stirring occasionally.
3. Shred chicken.
4. Serve over coconut rice or eat with tortillas.
