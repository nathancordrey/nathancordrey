# Ham Hash Brown Breakfast Casserole

A hearty breakfast casserole with hash browns, ham, eggs, and cheddar cheese. Great for a family breakfast, brunch, or feeding a crowd.

**Servings:** 12  
**Prep time:** 10 minutes  
**Cook time:** 45 minutes  
**Total time:** 55 minutes

Adapted from [The Country Cook's Ham Hash Brown Casserole](https://www.thecountrycook.net/ham-hash-brown-breakfast-casserole/) by Brandie Skibinski.

## Ingredients

- 1 (26-oz) bag frozen hash browns, thawed and patted dry
- 1 (8-oz) package diced ham (listed as 1 cup in the original recipe)
- 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese, divided
- 8 eggs
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- Nonstick cooking spray, for the baking dish

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Coat a 9 × 13-inch baking dish with nonstick cooking spray.
2. Spread the thawed, dried hash browns across the bottom of the dish. Layer the diced ham and **1 cup of the cheddar cheese** on top.
3. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, sour cream, heavy cream, salt, pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder until evenly combined.
4. Pour the egg mixture over the hash browns, ham, and cheese. Press down gently with a spatula to help the liquid reach the layers below.
5. Sprinkle the **remaining 1 cup of cheddar cheese** over the top.
6. Bake for about **45 minutes**, until the middle is set and the cheese is golden.
7. Let stand for **5 minutes** before cutting and serving.

## Notes

- **Dry the hash browns:** After thawing, pat them with paper towels to reduce excess moisture in the finished casserole.
- **Cheese:** The original recipe calls for 2 cups total, but you can add more if you prefer a cheesier casserole.
- **Ham quantity:** The source describes an 8-oz package as 1 cup. These are not necessarily equivalent by volume; using the full 8-oz package follows the stated package amount.
