# Caputo 00 Pizza Dough

A simple overnight pizza dough made with Caputo 00 flour. This version is adjusted for easier handling in a home kitchen while keeping notes for a wetter, more traditional 00-flour dough.

This recipe makes two 10- to 12-inch pizzas.

## Ingredients

- 250 g Caputo 00 flour
- 160 g water, for an easier-to-handle dough
- 5 g fine sea salt
- 1 g active dry yeast
- 2 g sugar
- Extra virgin olive oil, for lightly coating the bowl

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, stir the yeast and sugar into the water. Let it sit for a few minutes while you prepare the flour.
2. Add the flour to the bowl of a stand mixer.
3. Add the yeast mixture to the flour and mix on low speed until a rough dough forms.
4. Add the salt and continue mixing.
5. Switch to the dough hook and knead on low to medium-low speed for 8 to 10 minutes, until the dough is smooth and elastic.
6. Shape the dough into a ball and place it in a lightly oiled bowl. Cover and let it rest at room temperature for about 1 1/2 hours.
7. Divide the dough into two equal dough balls.
8. Place the dough balls in a lightly floured or lightly oiled covered container and refrigerate for 24 hours.
9. Remove the dough from the refrigerator about 1 1/2 to 2 hours before baking.
10. Place a baking stone or baking steel in the oven and preheat at the highest temperature your oven allows for about 1 hour.
11. Stretch each dough ball into a 10- to 12-inch pizza.
12. Add sauce, cheese, and toppings.
13. Bake until the crust is browned and the toppings are cooked.

## Notes

- This version uses 160 g water, which is about 64% hydration. It should be easier to stretch and less sticky than the original recipe.
- The Ditalia / Caputo-style version uses about 3/4 cup total water for 250 g flour, which is roughly 175 to 177 g water, or about 70% to 71% hydration. That can make a lighter, wetter dough, but it is harder to handle.
- For a wetter dough closer to the original, increase the water to 170 to 177 g after you are comfortable with the process.
- For a home oven, a 50/50 mix of Caputo 00 flour and bread flour is also worth trying. It can give a little more browning and chew.
- For an outdoor pizza oven or very hot oven, 100% Caputo 00 flour is an excellent choice.
- Sugar is included to help browning in a home oven.
- The dough may not double during the first room-temperature rest. That is okay because this recipe uses a small amount of yeast and develops flavor during the overnight cold proof.
- If you need a same-day version, skip the refrigerator and let the divided dough balls rise at room temperature for about 4 hours. Watch carefully so they do not overproof.

## Simple Topping Plan

For each pizza:

- 1/4 to 1/3 cup pizza sauce
- 3 to 4 oz mozzarella
- Parmesan, optional
- Basil, optional
- Olive oil, optional

Keep the toppings light so the crust bakes well.
