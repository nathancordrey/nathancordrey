# Summer Succotash

Great soup made with local lima beans.

## Ingredients
- 12 cups chicken broth
- 8 cups large lima beans
- 4 cups of corn (about 6 ears)
- 2 cans fire roasted tomatoes
- 1 tbs garlic
- salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in a pot.
2. Boil for 30 minutes.
3. Serve and enjoy.
