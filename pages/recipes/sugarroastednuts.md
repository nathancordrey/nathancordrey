# Sugar Roasted Nuts

Sweet, cinnamon-coated almonds and pecans made in the crockpot. Great for holiday gatherings and snacking.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 3 tbsp cinnamon
- 2 large pinches salt
- 1 egg white
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 1/2 cups almonds
- 2 1/2 cups pecans
- 1/4 cup water

## Instructions

1. Combine the white sugar, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt in a bowl.
2. Whip the egg white and add the vanilla extract.
3. Add the almonds and pecans to the egg-white mixture and stir to coat.
4. Add the dry ingredients to the nuts and mix well.
5. Spray the crockpot with cooking spray. Add the nuts, cover, and cook on **Low for 3 hours**, stirring every **30 minutes**.
6. After 3 hours, add the **1/4 cup water** and stir.
7. Continue cooking on **Low for 30 more minutes**, stirring every **15 minutes**.
8. Pour the nuts onto parchment paper and spread them out to cool.

## Notes

- Transcribed from the handwritten recipe. The final cooking stage is written as an additional 30 minutes, with stirring every 15 minutes.
